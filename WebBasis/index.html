PRESIDENT

Alejandro F. Diaz, MD.

A distinguished neurologist, educator, and senior medical consultant with extensive experience in vascular neurology and stroke care. He serves as a Full Professor and Senior Consultant at the University of Santo Tomas and currently leads the Philippine Society of Hypertension as its President. His work in clinical practice, medical education, research, and professional leadership reflects his commitment to advancing neurological and cardiovascular health in the Philippines.
